# 🚀 OMSC 1.0 IS HERE! 🎉

Streamers, meet your new stream companion! **OldMan Stream Companion is officially launching to the public — and it’s FREE!** 🥳

Your computer. Your stream. Your tools. Your data. No monthly subscription.

🎵 **Spotify & song requests** — manage your queue, build a community playlist and show off what’s playing with customisable animated widgets.
✨ **Twitch alerts with personality** — animated banners, your own images and media, custom sounds and video, and Channel Point triggered moments.
💬 **Native Twitch chat & commands** — plus a movable desktop chat overlay to keep your community close while you play.
🌟 **Celebrate your community** — shoutouts, raid welcomes and animated Stream Credits.
📊 **Make it yours** — local stream analytics, configurable modules, backup and restore, and an optional streamer directory.

Pick the tools that suit your stream, customise them, for OBS. OMSC runs locally on Windows, with direct Twitch and Spotify connections.

⬇️ **Download:** https://github.com/DeeJai1/OldMan-Stream-Companion-Public/releases/latest
🌐 **Explore the features:** https://oldmanstreamcompanion.com/

🛡️ **A quick Windows heads-up:** OMSC doesn’t currently have a publisher code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen may show an “unrecognised app” or unknown-publisher warning. That is separate from an antivirus detection. Find the latest public build’s dated scan report and SHA-256 file hashes at https://oldmanstreamcompanion.com/#release-security — investigate any malware detection.
